How to Identify Premium Quality Spirulina: Complete Buyer's Guide for Indian Market

Not all spirulina available in Indian market is created equal. Premium organic spirulina should have deep blue-green color, 65-70% protein content, and pass NABL heavy metal tests. Here's what Indian businesses should look for when sourcing bulk spirulina:

Premium Quality Indicators for Indian Market:

  • Color Test: Deep blue-green (not pale or yellowish indicates poor quality)
  • Protein Content: 65-70% minimum guaranteed (many Indian suppliers offer only 55-60%)
  • Moisture Content: Less than 7% for longer shelf life in Indian climate
  • Heavy Metals: Within WHO safety limits with NABL lab certificates
  • Indian Certifications: FSSAI, organic certification, ISO manufacturing standards
  • Packaging: Food-grade, moisture-proof packaging suitable for Indian conditions
